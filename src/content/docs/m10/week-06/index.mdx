Last week a list held the names of {chip.name}'s {chip.blocks.length} blocks, or the four corners of one rectangle. This week you put lists to work. You cut a piece out of a list, look for an element, and sort a list so that the biggest block comes first. Then you put lists inside a list, so that one variable holds every rectangle of the chip. The M10 capstone report needs all of this.

## Review these first

This week builds on three earlier lessons. If any of these feels shaky, spend five minutes on it before Lesson A:

- [Week 5, Lesson A](/SJTU-VLSI-TCL-Language/m10/week-05/lesson-a/): `llength`, `lindex` counting from 0, and rectangles as `{x1 y1 x2 y2}`.
- [Week 5, Lesson B](/SJTU-VLSI-TCL-Language/m10/week-05/lesson-b/): `foreach` and `lappend`. You use both in every example this week.
- [Week 3, Lesson A](/SJTU-VLSI-TCL-Language/m10/week-03/lesson-a/): braces keep a group together. A list inside a list is just a group in braces.
