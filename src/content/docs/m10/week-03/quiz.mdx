Ten questions: about 7 on this week (quoting and substitution) and 3 from Weeks 1 and 2. Answer without your notes. A quiz is practice, not a test: the struggle to remember is what makes the memory stronger.

- After each answer you see why the right answer is right, and why your choice was not.
- At the end you get your score and can read every explanation again.
- Every question you miss goes into your [spaced review](/SJTU-VLSI-TCL-Language/review/) deck, so it comes back in a few days.

Nothing is sent anywhere. Your score stays in this browser.
