Every script meets bad input sooner or later. This week yours learn to expect it: to refuse a bad value with a clear message, to catch the error, report it and carry on. Then you put all of M10 together in the capstone, a checker for the floorplan of {chip.name}, and finish with the M10 exam.

## Time plan (about 5 hours)

This week is longer than the others. If you need to, spread it over two weeks: the lessons and the quiz in the first, the capstone and the exam in the second.

| Step | Time | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| [Lesson A: error, catch and errorInfo](/SJTU-VLSI-TCL-Language/m10/week-10/lesson-a/) | 40 min | Your own error messages, catching errors, reading the stack trace |
| [Lesson B: try, finally and defensive procs](/SJTU-VLSI-TCL-Language/m10/week-10/lesson-b/) | 40 min | try … on error, finally, guard clauses |
| [Weekly quiz](/SJTU-VLSI-TCL-Language/m10/week-10/quiz/) and [spaced review](/SJTU-VLSI-TCL-Language/review/) | 20 min | 10 questions, then your review cards |
| [Capstone: Mini Floorplan Checker](/SJTU-VLSI-TCL-Language/m10/capstone/) | 2 to 3 h | The M10 project; see the [capstone week page](/SJTU-VLSI-TCL-Language/m10/week-10/lab/) |
| [M10 exam](/SJTU-VLSI-TCL-Language/m10/exam/) | 45 min | 30 items, mostly "what does this print?" |

Do the quiz before the capstone: it shows which ideas need another look while there is still time.

## Review these first

This week builds on three earlier lessons. If any of these feels shaky, spend five minutes on it before Lesson A:

- [Week 4, Lesson A](/SJTU-VLSI-TCL-Language/m10/week-04/lesson-a/): `if` and `else`. `if {[catch …]}` is an if like any other.
- [Week 7, Lesson A](/SJTU-VLSI-TCL-Language/m10/week-07/lesson-a/): `proc` and `return`. An error leaves a proc the way `return` does, but as a problem.
- [Week 7, Lesson B](/SJTU-VLSI-TCL-Language/m10/week-07/lesson-b/): `global`. A proc reads `errorInfo` with `global errorInfo`.
