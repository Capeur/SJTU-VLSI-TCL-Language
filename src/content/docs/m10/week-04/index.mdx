Until now, your scripts ran every line, once, from top to bottom. This week they learn to choose and to repeat. You check {chip.name}'s wires against the factory's rules, and you count how many wires fit between two blocks. Every later week uses these two ideas: the M10 capstone, the Mini Floorplan Checker, is built from them.

Try to put a day or two between the lessons and the quiz. A short gap makes the quiz harder, and that is exactly what makes it work.

## Review these first

This week builds on two earlier lessons. If either feels shaky, spend five minutes on it before Lesson A:

- [Week 2, Lesson A](/SJTU-VLSI-TCL-Language/m10/week-02/lesson-a/): `expr`, operators and the order of operations. Conditions are expr maths.
- [Week 3, Lesson B](/SJTU-VLSI-TCL-Language/m10/week-03/lesson-b/): Tcl substitutes only once, and braced `expr`. This explains why loops need braces.
